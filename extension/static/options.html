On this device

Your data, your choice

No account, cloud sync or analytics. Preferences stay on this device. Current plans, timing and up to 100 completed summaries use browser session memory by default. Optional encrypted history saves completed summaries while unlocked; it never gates the timer or viewing controls. Reflections and notes stay in extension pages. Chrysalis accesses YouTube page structure to implement controls without storing a video browsing history. It does not record video titles, URLs, search queries, transcripts or account identities. The latest 100 completed sessions are kept; older summaries are automatically removed. Notes are limited to 500 characters.
